## 🚀 Overview
When you're ready to move past basic editing, PowerDirector for Windows delivers the advanced toolset CyberLink is known for. Available in editions such as PowerDirector Ultra and PowerDirector Ultimate, it hands serious editors precise, frame-level control alongside a deep bank of professional effects, keyframing, and color grading. The PowerDirector video editor handles high-resolution footage smoothly and exports in quality suited to demanding projects, while its interface keeps advanced features within easy reach. Higher editions add more premium effects, plug-ins, and content, so you can pick the level of power your work requires. It's a capable choice for creators who want professional results on a Windows PC.

## ✨ Features
- **Precise, frame-level editing** — trim, keyframe, and fine-tune with the accuracy demanding projects require.
- **Professional effects** — access an extensive library of effects, transitions, and plug-ins, expanded further in higher editions.
- **Advanced color grading** — shape mood and consistency with detailed color and correction tools.
- **High-resolution output** — edit and export high-resolution video with smooth, hardware-accelerated performance.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is PowerDirector free?**
PowerDirector has a free version to get started, and paid editions such as Ultra and Ultimate add professional effects, plug-ins, and content. You can begin with the free download and upgrade to the edition that fits your work.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
PowerDirector runs on 64-bit Windows, primarily Windows 10 and Windows 11. A currently supported version is recommended, especially for high-resolution editing.

**What's the difference between Ultra and Ultimate?**
Both are advanced editions of PowerDirector; Ultimate typically includes additional premium effects, plug-ins, and content on top of what Ultra offers, giving you more creative options.

**Is my data secure?**
PowerDirector lets you edit locally on your Windows PC. Sign in with a trusted account, keep the app updated, and store your exported files like any other media on your computer.
